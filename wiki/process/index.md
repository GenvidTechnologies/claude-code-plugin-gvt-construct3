# Process contracts

Machine-read contracts consumed by `gvt-dev` skills. Unlike the concept pages in this
bundle, these are read **by path** by a skill — relocating one means updating the matching
`paths` override in `.gvt-agent.json`, not just a link.

* [Issue triage conventions](issue-triage.md) - The flat-label triage contract consumed by `/gvt-dev:triage-issues`, resolved through the `docs/issue-triage.md` override.

See the [wiki index](../index.md) for the rest of the bundle.
